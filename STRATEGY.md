# STRATEGY — "Receipts"

## The entry

One word: **Receipts**. The program writes it, byte for byte, to
`/output/entry.txt` (8 bytes, no trailing newline).

## Why this word wins each judge criterion (in the judge's order)

1. **A single word that describes the whole internet.** "Receipts" is the
   internet's memory. Everything posted is saved, screenshotted, archived
   and resurfaced years later; nothing is ever truly deleted. The word
   names that whole-system property, not one app or one moment.
2. **Funny by itself, no explanation needed.** "The internet keeps
   receipts" is a joke that lands cold: the moment you read the word you
   hear the threat — your old posts are filed away and will be produced
   against you. It makes the reader laugh and then nod.
3. **True about the internet as a whole.** Screenshots, archives,
   quote-tweets, Wayback, drama threads, old tweets dug up: the keeping
   of receipts is what the internet *does*, across every platform, in
   every language. It is not one app, one site or one mood.
4. **Surprising — not the first obvious word.** The first words anyone
   reaches for are doomscroll, viral, algorithm, loading, infinite,
   buffering. "Receipts" comes from slang, not from tech vocabulary, so
   it stands out in a field of tech words.
5. **Lands in one beat.** Eight letters, one common English word, spelled
   correctly, instantly readable. No setup, no decoding.

## What was tried and dropped

- **doomscroll** — the most obvious word in the field; fails criterion 4
  outright, and it describes a mood (one way of using the internet), not
  the internet as a whole (criterion 3).
- **viral / algorithm / loading / buffering / infinite** — all tech
  vocabulary, all dry. None earns a laugh by itself (criterion 2); they
  read as labels, not jokes. "Loading" and "buffering" are states, not
  descriptions of the whole internet.
- **Groupchat** — the strongest runner-up. Good on criteria 1, 3, 4, 5
  (the whole internet is one big group chat), but it is more descriptive
  than funny: it does not earn a laugh the way "Receipts" does, and
  criterion 2 is weighted second. Kept as the v2 candidate if the blind
  rounds rank "Receipts" below the paid places.
- **Screenshots** — true (everything gets screenshotted) but flat: no
  joke in the word itself, and 11 letters is a slower beat than 8.
- **Lore / Archive** — "Lore" is vague and trendy; "Archive" is dry and
  institutional. Neither is funny.

## Risks

- "Receipts" is slang; a judge that reads it literally (a paper slip from
  a shop) would miss the joke. The judge is briefed as a comedy editor
  who has read the entire internet, and "keeping receipts" is one of the
  internet's most widespread idioms, so the slang reading is the likely
  one.
- Another entrant may submit the same word. Byte-identical entries share
  one rate and the earlier receipt places above its copies — submit early.
